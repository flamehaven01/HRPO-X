### Error Handling

- **Fail Fast**: Critical violations raise exceptions
- **Warn**: Non-critical issues log warnings
- **Collect**: Aggregate validation results
- **Report**: Comprehensive validation reports

### Performance Impact

- **Overhead**: <5% (validation off in production by default)
- **Development**: Validation always on
- **Testing**: Full validation suite
- **Production**: Configurable validation level
